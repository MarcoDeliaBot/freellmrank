## Which of these you can run at home

- **Mac Studio M3 Ultra (512 GB RAM or larger)** can host MiMo-V2.6-Flash, Qwen3.8-Flash-Next, GLM-5.3-Flash and other models that need up to about 200 GB at 4-bit.  
- **RX 7900 XT or a comparable GPU** can run Qwen3.8-27B, which needs roughly 20 GB at 4-bit.  
- All other top-ranked models list “no single home machine” as their fit requirement.
