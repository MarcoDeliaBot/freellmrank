## What "best" does not tell you

The best free chat model is often a very large model. Large models are free to download, but you need a lot of GPU memory to run them. If you want the best model for *your* computer, use the [Run at home simulator](/home.html): pick your graphics card and it shows the highest-voted model that fits, with an estimated speed.

If you want to try them without installing anything, the [comparison page](/compare.html) sends one prompt to two free models at once, on your own free OpenRouter account.
