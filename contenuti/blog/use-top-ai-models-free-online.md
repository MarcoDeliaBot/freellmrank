You do not need a graphics card to use a strong free model. There are three ways.

## 1. The labs' own chat apps

Most labs that release open models also run a free chat website, with daily limits: for example Qwen, Kimi, GLM and DeepSeek. On every model page we link the official chat when there is one.

## 2. Free APIs through OpenRouter

OpenRouter gives access to many providers with one account. Today {{apigratis}} text models there cost nothing per token. Free models have rate limits per account: without any credit it is around 50 requests a day.

Our [comparison page](/compare.html) uses this: you connect your free OpenRouter account in one click, and your prompts go from your browser straight to OpenRouter. We never see your key or your messages.

## 3. Run it in your browser or at home

Small models can run on your own computer for free, with no limits and fully private. See [how much GPU memory you need](/blog/how-much-gpu-memory-for-a-local-llm.html), or go straight to the [Run at home simulator](/home.html).

## What "free" usually costs you

Free tiers exist to bring developers in. Some providers may use free-tier prompts to improve their models, so read the terms before sending anything private. Running a model at home is the only option where nothing leaves your computer.
